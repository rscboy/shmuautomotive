Membership FAQ

Unlimited means your enrolled vehicle can receive included full synthetic oil changes during the membership year whenever it has been driven at least 5,000 miles since the last included membership oil change.

The mileage requirement keeps service intervals healthy, prevents unnecessary oil changes, and helps the membership stay affordable while still protecting your engine.

Most passenger cars, SUVs, crossovers, and light-duty gas trucks within the Dayton service area qualify. Final approval is based on vehicle condition, oil capacity, filter availability, and service access.

Each included oil change covers up to 5 quarts of full synthetic oil. Vehicles that require more than 5 quarts can still enroll, but the extra oil is billed at cost per service. Shmu will confirm your vehicle's capacity during enrollment.

Diesel vehicles are not automatically included because oil capacity, filters, and service requirements vary. Submit the enrollment form and Shmu will confirm eligibility or quote the correct option.

No. Memberships are tied to the enrolled vehicle or vehicles and are non-transferable to other vehicles or owners.
