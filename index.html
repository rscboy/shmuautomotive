What I Do

The Driveway Oil Change

Strictly full synthetic. Standard, high-mileage, or premium Valvoline. I bring the oil, the filter, and the drain pan. Not a single drop left on your driveway.

Filters That Need Changing

Air and cabin filters. I'll show you the dirty one first. If it looks like a squirrel built a summer home in it, we replace it. If not, it stays.

Fluid Top-Offs

Windshield washer fluid, coolant, power steering. If it's low, I top it off while I'm in there. No sneaky "fluid service" fees attached.

Free Diagnostics

Check engine light glaring at you? I'll plug in the magic computer and tell you why your car is angry. Free with any oil change.

Battery Tests & Jumps

Because sometimes it's not the oil, it's just a dead battery. I can test it, jump it, or swap it right in your driveway.

Wipers & Bulbs

Streaky wipers or a burnt-out headlight? I'll swap them out while the oil drains so you can actually see where you're going.
